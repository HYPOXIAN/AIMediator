CURRENT PLAYER POSITION — decide this before choosing point direction:
- “Why is X STILL NOT nerfed?” expresses a desired nerf, even without a question mark: direction=nerf, stance=explicit_request. It does NOT mean “do not nerf X”.
- “Do not nerf X” vetoes a nerf: direction=keep.
- “X was nerfed and can no longer do Y” reports a past change. Without a separate new request: stance=observation, direction=unclear. NEVER label a new nerf request merely because the historical word “nerf” appears. A criticism of a previous nerf does not ask for another nerf.
Direction is the author's current position, not the direction of a past patch event. If scope, level and source meaning are clear but no new action is stated, keep the concrete observation with direction=unclear instead of inventing an action.

Decide INFORMATION LEVEL before emotional tone:
- A fighter name plus "broken", "too strong/weak", or an unspecific buff/nerf/keep request is GENERAL: segment content_kind=general_opinion, point detail_level=general. An explicit request is a STANCE, not proof of concrete information. A fighter name and the verbs buff/nerf are NOT mechanics.
- CONCRETE requires an actual combat mechanic, move, gameplay condition, interaction or specific experience stated in the source. A strength adjective or "please change this fighter" is never sufficient. concrete points use concrete_feedback segments.
- Prices, presentation, performance/network issues and creator/legal discussion are unrelated unless the source actually makes a competitive-balance claim. Pure insults without balance information are pure_emotion. Hostility inside informative criticism does not lower its information level or weight.

Decide SCOPE independently:
- A named fighter's strength or move concerns are character_balance. Copy the explicitly named fighter into character_literal; do not leave it null when the name is clear.
- Drive Rush/Impact, parry, controls and general competitive-system balance are system_balance. Such points need NO fighter name: character_literal=null. For example an explicit nerf request for Drive Rush is system_balance, explicit_request, with a source-literal mechanic; it is not an unidentified fighter request.
- Use uncertain for genuinely unresolved attribution, identity, context or irony. A missing mechanic in an otherwise clear general fighter opinion does NOT require uncertainty. Direction may be unclear while the information itself is certain.
